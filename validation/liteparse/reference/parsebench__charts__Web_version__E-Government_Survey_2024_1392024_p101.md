Overall, the legislative framework for advancing e-government is better developed in LLDCs, including those that are least developed, than in SIDS or LDCs (see figure 2.35). Most LLDCs have an e-government or digital government strategy (78 per cent), legislation or policy documents on cybersecurity (91 per cent), legal provisions for data protection (88 per cent), legislation governing freedom of information (78 per cent), and digital ID regulations (72 per cent). Fewer SIDS and LDCs (51 to 65 per cent) have these types of legislation in place, and the proportions are even lower for LDC/SIDS (between 13 and 65 per cent, depending on the type of legislation).
