# BAOTOU

Baotou is the largest city within the Inner

Mongolia Autonomous Region in China.

It's population of more than 1.6 million primarily consists of Han Chinese. Despite its location in Inner Mongolia, there are only 25,000 ethnic Mongols living in the city, in addition to 28,000 Hui Muslims,

18,000 Manchu, 650 Koreans, 550 Daur and

220 Xibe. Many of the street signs in Baotou are in the Mongolian script, but these are mainly for show only, as the population of the city is overwhelmingly

Han, and even many of the people classified as Mongols in the city have lost their cultural identity and mother tongue Baotou is situated on the banks of the Yellow River. Baotou means 'land with deer' in Mongolian, although deer have long been hunted to extinction in this part ofthe province. Baotou was a small, non-descript town clustered in the semi-arid desert until the 1950's, when the railway line from Baotou to Beijing and Yinchuan was constructed and good roads were built to enable to central government to mine the region's coal and iron deposits. Tens of thousands of workers flooded into Baotou, causing it to develop into a large industrialized city almost overnight. Today, Baotou is a geographically huge city.
